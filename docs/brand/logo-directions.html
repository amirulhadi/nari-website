NARI Studio · Brand exploration · Phase 1

Logo directions

Four early directions for the NARI Studio mark and wordmark. These are sketches to choose a path, not finished artwork: the chosen direction gets refined (optical balance, small-size versions, app-icon versions) in Phase 2. The wordmark is set in Manrope throughout. Accent: teal, carried over from the app.

A · Frame & Play

Product-literal

A slide frame with a forward "play" shape inside: the product in one glyph, from slide to playable course. Instantly reads as authoring and media.

Strengths
  • Says "slides + video + publish" without words
  • Very clear at favicon and taskbar sizes
Risks
  • Play-in-a-box is a common video-app trope
  • Doesn't contain the name, so it's weaker on recall

B · Path N

Recommended

An "N" drawn as one continuous learner path, ending in a teal node: a monogram for the name that also suggests branching, progress and a destination (completion). Ownable, and it scales from favicon to billboard.

NARI Studio

Strengths
  • Carries the initial, so it's good for recall and app icons
  • The story (a path to completion) fits learning and branching
  • Simple geometry, easy to animate subtly on the site
Risks
  • Monogram Ns are common, so the node and proportions must carry the identity
  • Needs careful optical tuning at 16 px

C · Layers

Abstract

Three stacked slides stepping forward: layers, sequence and a timeline. The front slide is the published, teal one. Calm and architectural, like a tool for building things.

D · Wordmark-first "nari"

Typographic

A lowercase custom wordmark: the "n" arch is the symbol, and a teal dot marks the moment of learning (it also echoes the dot of the "i"). It feels friendly, confident and modern, and leads with the name.
